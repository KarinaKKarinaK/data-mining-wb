## Design Context

### Users
Students learning data mining: reading explanations, working formulas by hand, and checking their answers in the labs. Long study sessions, laptop first, phone for flashcards.

### Brand Personality
Precise, calm, technical. A quiet instrument panel for studying: delicate, never loud or salesy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark graphite tinted toward teal. Periwinkle-indigo accent, cyan-teal secondary, beige for highlights. Soft indigo and teal glows, a faint dot grid, glass panels with a 1px edge at about 6% white. Delicate nodes and thin curved connections, as in a node-graph editor. No side stripes, no uppercase mono labels, no gradient text.
References: the DeepSeek V3 site (indigo to periwinkle) and dark teal node-graph app mockups.

### Design Principles
1. Content first: the formula, the chart and the worked numbers carry the page.
2. Tone over lines: tinted surfaces and spacing group things; hairlines only inside tables.
3. Every word on topic: no intros that restate the heading, no marketing lines.
4. Motion only for state changes, short and eased, off with reduced motion.
5. The accent is rare: links, the active item, and things to watch out for.
